# SoulMatch AI 访谈 Prompt

> 用于 AI 访谈工具（如 Marvin）的三段式配置，直接复制粘贴对应字段。

---

## ① What's the one thing you most need to learn?

```
Whether this user is a fanatic seed user who scores 9-10 on the SoulMatch concept (AI personality clone for deep social matching), and whether they are willing to pay and participate in product iteration.
```

---

## ② Who are you targeting?

```
Chinese college students (especially from 985/211 universities), aged 18-25, who have social or dating needs but are unsatisfied with existing tools like Tantan or Soul. Single or open to new connections.
```

---

## ③ Build an interview about...

```
We are validating a new social app concept called SoulMatch (灵魂匹配). The core idea: an AI builds a "soul profile" of the user through a situational short-drama questionnaire (not a traditional form), then acts as the user's agent to pre-screen potential matches for deep compatibility — before the user invests real social energy. We will show 6 prototype screens during the interview.

The 6 core features in the prototype:
1. 灵魂问卷 (Soul Questionnaire) — lifestyle scenario questions (e.g. "What do you do on weekends?"), AI analyzes answers in real time to build a personality model
2. AI 灵魂档案 (Soul Profile) — AI-generated personality portrait with a written summary, trait tags (e.g. INFJ, idealist), interest map, and a 94% matching accuracy score; user can import data from music/social apps
3. 发现推荐 (Discover) — AI pre-screened match cards with compatibility scores, soul-resonance tags, and swipe/pass actions
4. AI 匹配报告 (Match Report) — deep compatibility report per match: dimension scores, AI narrative paragraph, conversation topic suggestions, CTA to send a greeting
5. AI 聊天辅助 (AI Chat Assistant) — real-time conversation coaching in a 1-on-1 chat; AI suggests pre-written messages labeled with an "AI" badge; user can adopt them with one tap
6. 社交复盘 (Social Debrief) — post-conversation quality report: what went well, growth areas, progress bars, and next-step recommendations

Interview goals:
1. Understand their current social/dating pain points and unmet needs
2. Get their genuine reaction to the SoulMatch concept and prototype screens
3. Measure which of the 6 features resonates most and which feels unnecessary
4. Assess privacy comfort and willingness to pay
5. Identify if they qualify as a fanatic seed user (score 9-10, willing to join ongoing iteration)

Key topics to cover:
- Current social life: how they meet new people, what frustrates them most
- Experience with dating/social apps (Tantan, Soul, etc.): what they tried, why it failed
- Reaction to the soul questionnaire format: does situational drama feel more natural than a traditional form?
- Show Screen 2 (Soul Profile): does the AI-generated personality portrait feel accurate and useful? Would they trust it for matching?
- Show Screen 3 (Discover): does AI pre-screening feel helpful or intrusive?
- Show Screen 4 (Match Report): does the deep compatibility report make them more confident to reach out?
- Show Screen 5 (Chat Assistant): would they use AI-suggested messages? Does one-tap adoption feel natural or fake?
- Social debrief: "If AI analyzed your conversation after the fact — what went well, what to improve, and gave suggestions for next time — would that be valuable to you?"
- Privacy comfort: would they answer 20 soul questions? Import music taste or public social content (e.g. Weibo, Xiaohongshu)? What feels too invasive?
- Payment: which feature would they pay for? How much per month?
- Closing: overall score 1-10 for the concept, willingness to be a beta tester

Interview duration: 10-15 minutes. Conduct in Chinese (Mandarin). Be conversational and curious, not salesy. Dig into hesitations and surprises. If the user scores 9-10 and wants to join beta, flag them as a seed user candidate.
```

---

## 使用说明

1. 将以上三段内容分别填入访谈工具对应字段
2. 如工具支持上传图片，附上 `mvp/html/04_match_report.html` 截图作为原型展示素材
3. 真人访谈（第 1-2 个）结束后再切换为 AI 访谈批量投放
4. 种子用户判定标准：整体打分 ≥ 9 分 + 愿意参与后续迭代测试
5. 每次访谈约 10-15 分钟，首次访谈给用户 20-30 元报酬（金娜报销）
